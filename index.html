I’m a software engineer based in Greater Vancouver. At Browse AI, I build cloud services and data infrastructure for web extraction and automation.

My work spans distributed services, data infrastructure, and inference performance. I took part in Google Summer of Code with Intel OpenVINO, researched distributed workload performance at the University of Calgary, and led a multidisciplinary team at Divar.

I’m drawn to the details that shape how a system behaves: how work is scheduled, how data moves, and what happens when things fail.
